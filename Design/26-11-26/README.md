## Viewing

Start the local server from this folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 18765 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

Open [the design canvas](http://127.0.0.1:18765/). Drag to pan and scroll to zoom. **Fit all** shows the full layout. Zoom in to see the artboard thumbnails. Search the sidebar and click a result to center it.

Double-click an artboard or sidebar result to open its prototype. You can also focus it with the keyboard and press Enter. Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **Both** for paired designs, or use **Open original** to open the exported HTML in a separate tab.

## Chat history screens

`Proto-43` to `Proto-47` show the chat history side list (stories CH-1 to CH-6). The panel icon at the top of the left sidebar hides it, and the same icon on the narrow strip shows it again. These screens are interactive: use Play on the canvas.
